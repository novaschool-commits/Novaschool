Virtual Lab

Practicals students can actually run.

Experiments are checked and scored on the server, so a result reflects what the student really did. Teachers can assign them to a section and review each step.

Series circuit

Connect the wires and close the switch to light a bulb, then read the current.

Parallel circuit

Wire two branches and see how each bulb draws its own current.

Simple pendulum

Set the length and angle, time several swings and find the period.

These are the experiment types the lab engine supports. Students see an experiment once the school has published it.

Privacy and security

How accounts are protected.

  • Passwords are stored as one-way hashes, never as plain text.
  • Every request is checked against the account's role and permissions on the server.
  • Sign-ins expire after 12 hours, and an administrator can sign a user out everywhere.
  • Administrative actions are recorded in an audit log.

School settings

Logo and color branding aren't configurable from here yet — they're built into the site's design directly.

Examination authority status

Controls whether higher-grade (9–12, O/A Level) report cards are marked official-from-Nova-School or arranged through an external board. Grades 1–8 are always published online by the school regardless of this setting.

Academic settings

Academic years and terms are managed in the Organization tab. Grade bands are managed from the staff dashboard's Results page.

User settings

Roles, permissions, and staff accounts are managed in the Management tab.

Notification settings

Notifications are in-app only right now (bell icons for staff and admins). No email or SMS delivery is connected yet, so there's nothing to configure here beyond what already happens automatically.

Integration settings

No payment gateway, email service, or SMS provider is connected. Fees are tracked as claim-and-confirm (no live payment processing).

AI settings

No AI features (tutor, content generation, assessments) are built into NovaSchool yet. This is a placeholder for when that's planned.

Security settings

Session activity, suspicious logins, and force-logout live in the Security tab. Granular policy controls (password rules, session length) aren't configurable yet — they're fixed in the code.

Create an experiment

v1 uses a JSON config to define an experiment rather than a visual builder. Supported types: circuit (series — needs voltage, resistance, and a connections array) and circuit_parallel (needs voltage and a branches array, each with its own resistance and 2-item connections array), and pendulum (needs allowedLengths in metres and allowedAngles in degrees, each 20 or less; optional gravity, tolerancePercent, maxOscillations).
